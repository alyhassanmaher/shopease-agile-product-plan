# ShopEase: Agile, Scrum and DevOps Product Plan

Product and delivery plan for **ShopEase**, an e-commerce platform concept, built for the Agile, Scrum and DevOps course at ESLSCA University.

## What it covers
- **Product requirements** for the ShopEase platform
- **Scrum setup:** Product Owner, Scrum Master and Development Team roles, product backlog, sprint planning, reviews and retrospectives
- **Agile principles** applied to the project, based on the Agile Manifesto
- **DevOps design:** a CI/CD pipeline and the automation and collaboration practices between development and operations
